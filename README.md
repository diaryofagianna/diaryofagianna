# hi, i'm gianna ♡

i like making thoughtful little things on the internet — from visual ideas and illustrations to small digital experiences. i'm interested in design, code, languages, and all the little details that make everyday things feel special.

this is my little corner to share what i'm making, explore what i'm curious about, and keep track of ideas as they slowly come to life.

## about

i enjoy bringing different things together — a bit of creativity, a bit of technology, and a lot of curiosity.

i'm drawn to beautiful typography, thoughtful design, meaningful stories, and digital experiences that feel simple and lovely to use.

i'm always learning something new, trying things out, and finding inspiration in the everyday.

## currently creating

lately, i've been exploring small digital projects that bring design, storytelling, and technology together.

one of my recent projects is **fengtu yicang** — a bilingual digital heritage experience inspired by the history, architecture, and community stories of a historic granary in china.

[explore the project](https://fengtu-yicang.floot.app/) · [view the source](https://github.com/diaryofagianna/fengtu-yicang)
